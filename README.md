## 🏛️ Core Quantitative Modules | المحاور الكمية والمالية

### 1. 🇸🇦 Saudi Equities Desk & Live Quant Radar (تاسي والفرص الذكية)
- **Official Blue Chips Feed:** Grounded in verified Tadawul closing prices (Saudi Aramco `25.74 SAR`, Al Rajhi Bank `62.35 SAR`, ACWA Power `166.10 SAR`, SABIC `46.50 SAR`, stc `42.60 SAR`, etc.).
- **Smart Signal Radar (رادار الفرص الكمية اللحظي):** Real-time opportunity screener identifying:
  - ⚡ **Oversold Rebound:** RSI below 35 for mean-reversion dip buying.
  - 💎 **DCF Undervaluation:** Fundamental intrinsic discount vs spot price.
  - 💰 **High Dividend Yield:** Blue chips offering > 4.5% annual cash payouts.
  - 🚀 **Golden Cross Momentum:** Bullish technical breakout acceleration.
- **Head-to-Head Stock Comparator (مقارنة الأسهم وجهاً لوجه):** Side-by-side benchmarking matrix for 2 or 3 stocks across fundamentals, DCF intrinsic value, 12M target forecasts, P/E, Beta, RSI, and quantitative consensus score.

### 2. 💼 Institutional Paper Trading Desk (محفظة التداول الافتراضي)
- **100,000 SAR Virtual Cash Wallet:** Risk-free simulated order execution desk.
- **Order Execution Ticket:** Instant Market BUY / SELL order placement with real-time slippage-free execution against live Tadawul prices.
- **Active Positions & Holdings Table:** Tracks shares owned, average cost basis, current market value, and unrealized P&L in SAR with quick liquidation.
- **Full Audit Trail:** Every transaction is cryptographically timestamped and persisted in `localStorage`.

### 3. 💰 Dividend & DRIP Compounding Calculator (حاسبة دخل التوزيعات و DRIP)
- **Cash Flow Simulation:** Projects annual and quarterly dividend cash flows in SAR.
- **10-Year Wealth Trajectory Chart:** Canvas visualizer comparing **DRIP Reinvestment** vs **Cash Withdrawal** over 5, 10, and 15-year horizons.
- **Compounding Alpha Metric:** Exact SAR wealth advantage generated by reinvesting corporate dividend payouts.

### 4. 📊 Modern Portfolio Theory & Custom Builder (ماركويتز وتوزيع الأوزان)
- **Markowitz Mean-Variance Optimization:** Analytical calculation of Tangency Portfolio (Max Sharpe) and Global Minimum Variance (GMV) portfolio via Two-Fund Separation Theorem.
- **Interactive Portfolio Builder:** Real-time asset allocation sliders across Saudi blue chips with 4 strategy presets (*Balanced, Max Dividends, High Growth, Defensive*) and instant annual cash dividend forecast.

### 5. ⚡ Derivatives Engineering Lab (معمل المشتقات والخيارات)
- **Black-Scholes-Merton Analytical Pricing:** European option pricing benchmarked against John C. Hull (10th Ed.).
- **First & Second-Order Greeks:** Real-time computation of $\Delta$ (Delta), $\Gamma$ (Gamma), $\Theta$ (Theta), $\mathcal{V}$ (Vega), and $\rho$ (Rho).
- **Cox-Ross-Rubinstein (CRR) Binomial Trees:** Discrete lattice engine for American options with early exercise boundary evaluation.
- **Implied Volatility (IV) Inversion:** High-precision Newton-Raphson & Brent solvers.
- **Multi-Leg Strategy Architect:** Payoff diagrams for Iron Condor, Bull Call Spread, Straddle, and Protective Collar.

### 6. 🎲 Monte Carlo Risk Simulation & VaR (محاكاة مونت كارلو وإدارة المخاطر)
- **Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM):** 500 stochastic price trajectories with drift and volatility.
- **Value at Risk (VaR 95% & 99%):** Parametric and historical risk forecasting.
- **Conditional VaR (CVaR / Expected Shortfall):** Tail risk risk assessment beyond the VaR threshold.
